Privacy policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

This policy describes how Knowverage handles personal data under Rwanda's Law No. 058/2021 relating to the protection of personal data and privacy. It is a working version and will be reviewed before final publication.

1. Who we are

Knowverage is a Kigali-based company that helps businesses get found, trusted and chosen online. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Knowverage. You can contact us at careers.knowverage@gmail.com or on +250 787 060 332.

2. What we collect

When you request a free audit or contact us, we collect the details you choose to give us:

  • Your name and business name
  • Your WhatsApp number or email address
  • Links to your website, social media pages or Google listing
  • Your main business goal and any package you showed interest in

If analytics are enabled, we also collect anonymous information about how visitors use the website, such as pages viewed and buttons clicked.

3. Why we use it

  • To prepare and send your free audit
  • To reply to your enquiry and follow up about our services
  • To understand which parts of the website help visitors, so we can improve it

We use your contact details only with your consent, which you give when you tick the box on our form. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the website and receive form submissions, such as our form-handling and email services, and analytics tools if enabled. They process data only to provide these services. Some of them may store data outside Rwanda, and we only use providers that protect personal data appropriately.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we need them for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete them.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Email careers.knowverage@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as possible.

7. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
